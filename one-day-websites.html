Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the One Day Websites service.

Can you really build a website in one day? ↓
Yes, for eligible projects. We use established, industry-specific website foundations and customize them with your business information. The one-day build begins once we have everything needed to start.
Will my website look like everyone else's? ↓
We tailor the branding, content, imagery, and relevant sections to your business. The underlying design system is established, but the final presentation reflects your company.
What do you need from me? ↓
Your business details, services, contact information, logo, and any relevant photos or branding materials. We'll guide you through the essentials.
Can I request changes? ↓
Yes. The number and scope of revisions depend on your selected package. We'll explain what's included before the project begins.
Will my website work on mobile? ↓
Yes. Responsive design is a core part of the service.
Is hosting and a domain included? ↓
Hosting and domain arrangements depend on the package. We'll confirm what's included before work starts.
Can I add more pages later? ↓
Yes. Your website can be expanded with additional pages and features as your business grows.
Is this suitable for every type of business? ↓
One Day Websites is designed primarily for straightforward local service business websites. Complex custom functionality, large content requirements, and specialized integrations may require a different project scope.
