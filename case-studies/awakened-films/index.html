THE CHALLENGE

A Video-Heavy Website With Technical Constraints.

Awakened Films' website needed to showcase the quality of its video production work. That meant incorporating rich video content throughout the site, including on important service and portfolio pages.

But a media-heavy website introduces practical challenges. Large video files can increase page weight, affect loading performance, and make the browsing experience more demanding—particularly on mobile devices.

The website also used a combination of WordPress and custom-coded elements. Some parts were hard-coded, limiting how easily they could be edited through the usual WordPress interface.

Our task was to improve the website and its search strategy while working within these technical constraints.

THE WORK / CORE DELIVERABLES

Design, Search, and Conversion—Working Together.

01
WordPress Website Redesign
We worked within the site's existing WordPress and custom-coded structure to improve the website's presentation and user experience. Rather than treating the project as a simple template replacement, we had to account for hard-coded sections, existing functionality, and the site's extensive use of video. The goal was to create a more cohesive experience while preserving the visual storytelling that defines Awakened Films.
02
Technical & Video-Heavy Page Improvements
The website relies on video to demonstrate the company's creative capabilities. We worked around the practical challenges of maintaining a media-rich website, focusing on how its pages were structured, presented, and experienced by visitors. The challenge was to balance an engaging visual experience with the usability needed for a professional B2B website.
03
SEO Strategy & Keyword Tracking
We developed an SEO strategy around relevant commercial searches for video production services. This included keyword research, mapping search opportunities to appropriate pages, tracking search positions, and identifying opportunities to strengthen the site's visibility. We monitored keyword rankings over time to understand which areas were improving and which needed further attention.
04
Content Planning & SEO Content
We created a structured content plan covering commercial video production, corporate video services, industry-specific production topics, and educational search queries. The strategy included both blog content and service-page opportunities. Our goal was to help Awakened Films build topical relevance and reach potential clients at different stages of their research and buying journey.
05
Microsoft Clarity & Conversion Analysis
After website improvements were introduced, we used Microsoft Clarity to study visitor interactions. We reviewed how users engaged with important pages, where they clicked, and how visitors interacted with the site's calls to action. These observations informed ongoing reviews of landing-page usability and conversion opportunities.
